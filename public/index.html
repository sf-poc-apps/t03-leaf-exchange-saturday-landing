Neighbourhood plant swap

Leaf Exchange Saturday

Bring a plant, take a plant home.

  • When Saturday, 10:00
  • Where Community Garden

How it works

  1. 1

    Bring a plant

    Pot up a cutting, seedling or spare plant from your windowsill or garden.

  2. 2

    Swap at the table

    Place your plant on the swap table and pick any other plant in exchange.

  3. 3

    Take it home

    Chat with neighbours about care tips, then carry your new plant home.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a plant to take part?
Ideally yes, but first-timers are welcome to look around and say hello. Somebody always has spare seedlings.
What kinds of plants can I bring?
Houseplants, herbs, vegetables, seedlings and cuttings. Just make sure pots are labelled and pest-free.
How many plants can I swap?
Take home as many plants as you brought. If you bring three, you can pick three.
What if my plant is not looking great?
Come anyway! Someone will happily give it a good home and a bit of care advice.
Is there a cost to join?
No. The swap is free and run by neighbours for neighbours. No money changes hands.
